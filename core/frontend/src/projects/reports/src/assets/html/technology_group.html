Automation Case Studies
Case Study 1: Invoice Management System
Solution: 

  • Our in-house software solution “Invoice Management System (IMS)” enables invoice processing without defects at such a high productive throughput freeing up the processor’s time.

  • The critical review feature of IMS is beneficial to the provider as the system identifies errors and omissions in the claims, such as missing information or incorrect client details.

  • IMS also helped to ensure that the claims met the NDIS funding criteria, reducing the risk of claims being rejected or delayed.

Result: 

  • Overall, the software solution enabled the NDIS service provider to significantly improve their claims filing process. 

  • The IMS unites information, processes, and associates to help automate digital workflows on premises.

  • The critical review feature helped to reduce errors and omissions, and the provider was able to file claims more quickly, leading to improved cash flow and better overall management of their claims process.

  • Reports and dashboards giving an overview of real time data pertaining to invoice processing.

 

Case Study 2: Job Management CRM

We developed the Job Management CRM, a centralized workflow automation for job allocation and review. The CRM enables the client to assign jobs and review completed jobs with processed SOA/ROA checklists. Project managers can see team members progress in the field in real time as they check off their task lists. Each time a new job is created automations can be setup with task lists for project-based work. With an intuitive interface, team collaboration is easy for the client, reviewer and processor

 

Solution: 

  • Streamlined Job Management: The CRM enables client to add and allocate jobs to the processor. Multiple jobs can be managed, and tasks can be assigned for new jobs. Client and Reviewer can track progress on the CRM.

  • Review Processed Jobs: Enables reviewers to review the jobs of the processor and give feedback on the defects.

  • Checklist Management: Checklist automated for the SOA/ROA job. Enables the processor to update the checklist for the processed job, and reviewer to track the progress and capture feedback.

  • Dashboards and Reporting: Dashboard and reports to identify the best actions to resolve cases quickly and improve collaboration between business teams and clients.

  • Incorporate Feedback and Instruction: Incorporates feedback and special instructions to ensure defect-free processing.

  • Client Management: Manage client (entity) information to track the job with different checklists for future reference.

  • Team Management: Project managers can see team members progress as they process the invoice. Each time a new invoice is processed automations can be set up with review alerts to the managers.

  • Capture Meeting Notes: Enables Processor to capture review meeting details such as Meeting date, location, Goals, Attendees and Discussion Points to action upon

 

Case Study 3: Checklist Automation

We developed the Global Checklist, a workflow automation for creating customized checklists to process and review accounting, audit jobs across all business teams. The automated checklist can be used for jobs where complex or multi-step processes are involved. Checklists can be automated for Reviewers and Processors based on the Process (Admin, Compliance, and Implementation) and Sub-Process (SOA, ROA, Calculation, Review Reports and No Advice)

Features

  • Create customized checklist based on the user type, process and sub-process.

  • Centralized document management for job processing.

  • Maintain checklist versioning.

  • Track and review job status updated by processors.

  • Update reviewer response and display response history in the checklist .

  • Highlight checklist questions disagreed by the reviewer.

  • Display Job Review Time.

  • Enable checklist report downloads.
