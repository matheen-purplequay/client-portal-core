Touch Point Status
  • In Progress: Assigned during initial review of financial documents, compliance check and gathering relevant information.
  • Query: Assigned when discrepancies, inconsistencies are identified or require additional information.
  • Draft Workpaper (DWP): Assigned when findings and recommendations are combined into preliminary documentation.
  • Revised Draft Workpaper (RDWP): assigned when revisions are made to the initial draft workpapers during the review process.
  • Audit: Indicates the completion of the pre-audit stages, including the review of workpapers and resolution of queries.
