Financial Planning

Carisma’s 360-degree financial advisory service has helped dozens of advisors by providing a collaborative platform that unifies reporting, analysis, simulation, controlling, planning and workflow capabilities. It helps you focus on your client base and business continuity by consolidating all value streams under one roof. Our team of experienced and qualified paraplanners will closely work with you to develop your business assuring you of accuracy and accountability throughout the process.

Financial advisors will recommend certain strategies to their end clients . End clients are individuals across Australia not any organisations.
Since Australia is a country which has got very strict compliance legislations, financial advisors cannot recommend strategies to client orally. They must prepare detailed compliance report for their recommendation , which we will call as Statement of Advice.(SOA).

Financial advisors will share the recommend strategies with us and we have to develop content for these strategies by doing proper Research, analysis of data, logical thinking, thinking outside box, with excellent verbal and written communication skill. We will have to go for calls with financial advisors daily and need to present our research findings . Once Advisor approved our findings , we have to prepare compliance report and submit. 

Once compliance report is finalised advisor will come back to us for implementing strategies where we have to make calls to service providers across Australia.

In short, we are doing financial planning for individuals across Australia through financial Advisor.
